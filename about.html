About the Catholic Church

Learn about the Catholic faith and its mission.

What is the Catholic Church?

The Catholic Church is a Christian community centered on Jesus Christ and the Gospel. Catholics follow the teachings of Christ and seek to live lives of faith, hope and love.

Our Mission

The mission of the Church is to proclaim the Gospel, celebrate the sacraments, serve others and lead people closer to God.

Our Faith

Catholic faith is rooted in Sacred Scripture, Sacred Tradition and the teaching authority of the Church.
