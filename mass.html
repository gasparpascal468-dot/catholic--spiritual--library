Mass & Liturgy

Learn about the celebration of the Holy Mass.

⛪

The Holy Mass

The Holy Mass is the central celebration of Catholic worship. Catholics gather to hear God's Word and participate in the Eucharistic celebration.

📖

Liturgy of the Word

The Liturgy of the Word includes readings from Sacred Scripture, the responsorial psalm, the Gospel and the homily.

🍞

Liturgy of the Eucharist

During the Liturgy of the Eucharist, the Church celebrates the Eucharistic Prayer and receives Holy Communion.

🙏

Participation

The faithful participate through prayer, listening, responses, singing and receiving Holy Communion when properly prepared.

🕊️

Liturgical Seasons

The Catholic liturgical year includes seasons such as Advent, Christmas, Lent, Easter and Ordinary Time.
