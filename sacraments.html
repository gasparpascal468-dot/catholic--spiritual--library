The Seven Sacraments

Signs of God's grace in the life of the Church.

💧 Baptism

Baptism is the sacrament through which a person becomes a member of the Christian community.

🕊️ Confirmation

Confirmation strengthens the baptized person through the gift of the Holy Spirit.

🍞 Eucharist

The Eucharist is the sacrament in which Catholics celebrate and receive Christ.

❤️ Reconciliation

This sacrament offers forgiveness and reconciliation with God.

💍 Matrimony

Matrimony is the sacramental covenant between a man and a woman.

🙏 Holy Orders

Holy Orders is the sacrament through which the Church's ordained ministry is conferred.

🕯️ Anointing of the Sick

This sacrament brings spiritual strength and comfort to people who are seriously ill.
